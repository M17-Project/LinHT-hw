# LinHT Side Buttons

A small side PCB for the [LinHT](https://github.com/M17-Project/LinHT-hw)
handheld transceiver, carrying the two side buttons (PTT area) of the radio.

The board is soldered edge-on (perpendicularly) to the side of the LinHT main
PCB. Its outline matches the original side-button board of the **Retevis C62**
donor radio, so it fits the stock enclosure and rubber button covers without
modifications.

## Bill of Materials

Apart from the PCB itself you need:

* 2× metal button dome (snap dome), e.g.
  [AliExpress item 33001419494](https://www.aliexpress.com/item/33001419494.html)

The domes are placed over the exposed button contacts and held in place by
adhesive tape or the button membrane.

## Fabrication

Gerber files are generated by the CI and published on the
[LinHT hardware page](https://linht-radio.eu/), or can be exported
locally from `side-pcb.kicad_pcb` with KiCad 10.
